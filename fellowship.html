Founding Fellowship Program

Business Development & Outreach Fellowship

Role Overview

ZMP Labs helps businesses use powerful AI without sending private files to the cloud. In fields like healthcare, law, and finance, professionals want to use AI to speed up their work, but uploading sensitive patient data or private financial files to public tools like ChatGPT creates major privacy and legal risks. We empower these teams to process data safely using private AI software that runs 100% offline on local computers.

As a fellow, you will work directly with founder Dr. C. Alan Jones to reach out to business executives and introduce our private AI solutions across healthcare, legal, and finance sectors.

What You Will Do

  • Send direct messages on LinkedIn and email to executives in healthcare, legal, and finance using our step-by-step outreach playbooks.
  • Connect with business owners, managing partners, and security leads who need safe, private AI tools for their teams.
  • Screen interested prospects and book 30-minute deployment consultations on Dr. Jones's calendar.
  • Keep track of your messaging results to see which industry responds best.

Structure & Performance Commission

This is a flexible, part-time project (5–10 hours/week) designed to give you real-world business development experience and valuable portfolio credentials for your resume. Fellows earn a 15% performance commission ($375 for every signed $2,500 client pilot), with opportunities for ongoing roles as ZMP Labs scales.
