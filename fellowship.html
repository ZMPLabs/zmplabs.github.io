Role Overview

ZMP Labs is expanding its go-to-market initiative for our zero-telemetry AI vault. In high-stakes fields—such as healthcare, legal services, M&A advisory, finance, and corporate compliance—professionals are under immense pressure to adopt AI, but face massive risks when uploading PHI, privileged client files, or unreleased financials into cloud LLMs. We empower these firms to process client data safely with a fully private, local AI built for regulated industries.

In this role, you will work directly with founder Dr. C. Alan Jones to introduce our local-first AI vault to decision-makers across healthcare, legal, and financial tech sectors.

  • Screen inbound interest and book 30‑minute deployment consultations on Dr. Jones’s calendar.
  • Track outreach metrics to identify which regulated niche shows the highest conversion rates.
