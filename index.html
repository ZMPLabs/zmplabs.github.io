A Note on the Origin of ZMPLabs

From the Founder

If you've spent any time in advisory work, you've probably felt that subtle weight in the pit of your stomach. Your team starts adopting modern AI tools to stay competitive, or you sign off on a cloud vendor to process sensitive client files. The data flows to their servers—somewhere you've never seen. And somewhere in the back of your mind, you know the data retention policy was buried on page fourteen of an agreement no one had time to read twice. Then you see the headlines—another platform, another breach, another hollow email offering twelve months of credit monitoring as though that closes the loop.

That unease is what started ZMPLabs. Not a catastrophe of my own, but the slow realization that the cloud era quietly shifted all the liability onto us—onto anyone whose name is on the letterhead when a client asks what happened to their information.

That instinct to interrogate broken systems led me through a doctorate at Walden University, where I studied how policy design creates or removes barriers for real people—specifically, how African American men navigate the benefits and obstacles of financial assistance programs1. Earning my Ph.D. in Public Policy and Administration confirmed something I now build software around: broken systems rarely fail the people who designed them. They fail the people downstream. When your client data lives on infrastructure you don't control, you are downstream.

ZMPLabs exists so you never have to choose between leveraging modern AI capabilities and upholding your fiduciary duty. Local-first architecture ensures your data stays yours—every single bit of it, running strictly on hardware you physically control. Not just because it's elegant engineering, but because when a client asks where their data lives, you should be able to look them in the eye and give them an unmistakable answer.

Dr. C. Alan Jones

Founder, ZMPLabs

ZMPLabs Architecture Brief

Secure AI for Compliance-Restricted Workflows

1. The Threat Model: Cloud AI Vulnerabilities

Consumer and public cloud AI models introduce massive legal liabilities for regulated industries. Public cloud interfaces operate with persistent data retention to train future generative models and monitor user behavior. Pasting unredacted client financial records, patient diagnostics, or legal briefs into these systems actively breaks data residency rules and exposes firms to catastrophic data leaks and compliance breaches.

2. The ZMP Architecture: Zero-Metadata Processing

ZMP (Zero-Metadata Protocol) is a local-first enterprise architecture designed to eliminate third-party cloud exposure entirely:

  • • Local Execution: AI model weights run directly and securely on existing executive hardware.
  • • Memory-Only Processing: Prompts and outputs are processed strictly in system RAM and destroyed upon session closure.
  • • Zero Persistent Logging: Completely bypasses persistent disk logging; no query history is stored or transmitted.
  • • Zero-Code Interface: Intuitive desktop chat UI requiring zero coding or technical expertise.
3. Regulatory Mapping & Frameworks

Maintains strict adherence to mandatory professional codes and privacy frameworks:

• HIPAA & HITECH: Processes PHI locally without requiring third-party Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).
• ABA Model Rule 1.6: Preserves attorney-client privilege by eliminating third-party server transmissions.
• SEC Reg S-P & IRC 7216: Safeguards non-public financial records and taxpayer data locally.
• GDPR & CCPA / CPRA: Enforces absolute data residency with zero cross-border telemetry.
4. Deployment Specifications & SLA
  • • 48-Hour Turnaround: Bypasses traditional 4-to-6-month enterprise infrastructure lead times.
  • • 60-Minute Live Call: Pre-flight staging allows live installation and Wireshark isolation testing in 60 minutes.
  • • Hardware Scope: Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Windows laptops with discrete NVIDIA RTX 3060+ GPUs. Zero new server hardware required.
  • • Compliance Handoff: Includes a finalized, custom IT Governance Policy ready for immediate compliance review.
