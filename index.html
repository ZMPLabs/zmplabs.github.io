A Note on the Origin of ZMPLabs

From the Founder

Like many of you, I grew tired of having my personal information exposed in multiple corporate data leaks. I experienced the frustration of receiving vague notices about joining class-action lawsuits—never knowing if they were actually legitimate—and being offered a "free year of credit monitoring" as a hollow apology from the very companies that were supposed to protect my data.

My background is rooted in public policy and advocacy. During my doctoral research at Walden University, I studied how policy design impacts marginalized groups, specifically focusing on the benefits and barriers African American men experience with financial assistance programs[cite: 1]. Earning my Ph.D. in Public Policy and Administration taught me how deeply systemic vulnerabilities can impact real people[cite: 1].

I built ZMPLabs because I realized those same systemic vulnerabilities exist in how the modern workforce handles sensitive client data with cloud AI. You shouldn't have to choose between leveraging modern technology and keeping your clients' data secure. We built this local-first architecture so you never have to send another apology letter for a breach you couldn't control.

Dr. C. Alan Jones, Ph.D.

Founder, ZMPLabs
