A Note on the Origin of ZMPLabs

From the Founder

Like many professionals, I grew frustrated watching sensitive client data flow through third-party cloud services with opaque retention policies. I've seen the aftermath of data leaks—vague breach notifications, hollow credit monitoring offers from companies that failed to protect what was entrusted to them.

My background is rooted in public policy and advocacy. During my doctoral research at Walden University, I studied how policy design impacts marginalized groups, specifically focusing on the benefits and barriers African American men experience with financial assistance programs. Earning my Ph.D. in Public Policy and Administration taught me how deeply systemic vulnerabilities can impact real people.

ZMPLabs exists because you shouldn't have to choose between leveraging modern AI capabilities and maintaining fiduciary duty to your clients. Local-first architecture means you control every byte.
